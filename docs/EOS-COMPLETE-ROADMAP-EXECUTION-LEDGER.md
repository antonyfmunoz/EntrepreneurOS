# EOS Complete Roadmap Execution Ledger

Date: 2026-08-26

## Purpose

This ledger is the release-level completion contract for EntrepreneurOS. It
prevents repository implementation, local qualification, production promotion,
provider authorization, field operation, replication, and native-authority
cutover from being collapsed into one ambiguous `complete` label.

The active objective is to complete every gate in this ledger. A gate remains
open until its named evidence exists for the exact subject and environment.
Synthetic fixtures, documentation, local tests, placeholder configuration, and
unattributable screenshots do not satisfy production or field gates.

## Source authority

1. Current Notion canon resolves product-intent contradictions.
2. Google Drive is supporting architecture where it is compatible with Notion.
3. The repository is the authority for implemented behavior.
4. Protected CI is the authority for the exact reviewed commit and image.
5. The deployed runtime, provider receipts, professional dispositions, and field
   Evidence are the authority for production and operating claims.
